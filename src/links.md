# Resources

## Papers

Two preprints describe the CogNarr ecosystem and the story graph meaning representation it is built on.

- John C. Boik. *CogNarr Ecosystem: Facilitating Group Cognition at Scale.* arXiv:2407.18945, 2024. [arxiv.org/abs/2407.18945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18945)
- John C. Boik. *CogNarr Ecosystem: Preliminary Thoughts on a Story Graph Meaning Representation.* OSF Preprints, 2024. [osf.io/muc29](https://osf.io/muc29/)

## Earlier work

Earlier work by John Boik on the de novo design of core societal systems and the Local Economic Direct Democracy Association (LEDDA) framework. The About page describes how this line of work led to CogNarr.

- John C. Boik. *Economic Direct Democracy: A Framework to End Poverty and Maximize Well-Being.* 2014. (book)
- John C. Boik. *Science-Driven Societal Transformation, Part I: Worldview.* Sustainability 12(17), 6881, 2020. [mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/17/6881](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/17/6881)
- John C. Boik. *Science-Driven Societal Transformation, Part II: Motivation and Strategy.* Sustainability 12(19), 8047, 2020. [mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/19/8047](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/19/8047)
- John C. Boik. *Science-Driven Societal Transformation, Part III: Design.* Sustainability 13(2), 726, 2021. [mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/2/726](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/2/726)
- John C. Boik. *First Micro-Simulation Model of a LEDDA Community Currency-Dollar Economy.* International Journal of Community Currency Research, 2014. [ijccr.net/article/view/9186](https://www.ijccr.net/article/view/9186)
